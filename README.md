# Bitcoin-Valuator

Bitcoin Valuator is a simple and minimalistic app designed to quickly estimate the value of your Bitcoin — fully private and local.

All your inputs are processed directly on your device. No accounts, no tracking, no data collection. Your data stays yours.

With a clean and intuitive interface, you can:
	•	Enter your Bitcoin amount
	•	Input the current BTC price
	•	Simulate a future BTC price
	•	Instantly calculate and compare values

The app focuses on what matters most: privacy, speed, and clarity. There are no unnecessary features, no distractions, and no complicated charts — just a straightforward tool to understand your Bitcoin value in seconds.

Perfect for anyone who values simplicity and privacy.
